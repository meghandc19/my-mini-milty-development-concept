# Asset Credits and Rights Notes

## Included in this public concept package

- `icon.svg` — simple original vector icon created for this concept package.
- Character, enemy, weapon, projectile, platform, background, and grass-cap visuals — basic original `Polygon2D` placeholder geometry in Godot scene files.
- No third-party raster artwork, music, or sound effects are included.

## Excluded pending verification

Previous raster character images, grass texture, a full-map image, and their associated map-specific collision geometry were excluded because this archive did not contain source/license records sufficient to verify public redistribution rights.

## Future asset rule

Before adding external assets, record the asset name, creator/source, URL, license, attribution text (if required), modifications, and date verified. Do not redistribute assets whose license or permission is unclear.
